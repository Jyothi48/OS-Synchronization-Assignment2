Notes

The monitor uses a Condition to protect philosopher states. A philosopher eats only when neither neighbour is eating, so adjacent philosophers cannot eat simultaneously. Condition-based coordination prevents deadlock in this design. Fairness and starvation depend on scheduling and repeated contention.
